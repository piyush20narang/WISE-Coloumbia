Our Core Devotional Programs

sa vai puṁsāṁ paro dharmo
yato bhaktir adhokṣaje
ahaituky apratihatā
yayātmā suprasīdati

The supreme occupation [dharma] for all humanity is that by which men can attain to loving devotional service unto the transcendent Lord. Such devotional service must be unmotivated and uninterrupted to completely satisfy the self.

Bhakti Vriksha Program

  • Systematic study of spiritual knowledge and wisdom through Bhagavad Gita, Ramayana, Srimad Bhagavatam and other Vedic Scriptures based on the teachings by His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Srila Prabhupada, the Founder Acarya of ISKCON.

  • Principles of Vedic Scriptures are discussed through interactive sessions which enable one to dive deeper to find its application in our daily lives.

Spiritual Retreat Sessions

Our rejoice with Krishna retreats provide an immersive experience for spiritual growth and community building.

  • Spiritual discourses by Motivational and Spiritual Seekers.

  • Serving of sanctified sumptuous vegetarian feast (Prasadam).

Hari Bol Beats
